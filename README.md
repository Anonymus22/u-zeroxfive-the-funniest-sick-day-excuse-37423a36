A one-line sick-day excuse for the make.fun bounty "The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse". The program writes a single funny, half-believable excuse — a smart lock that updated itself overnight and locked its owner out in their pyjamas — to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Sorry, can't come in today — my smart lock updated overnight, forgot my face, and now I'm locked out of my own flat in my pyjamas.
